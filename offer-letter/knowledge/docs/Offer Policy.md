# Aventura Frontiers Offer Policy

> **Sample data for a training build.** Every grade, pay range, allowance and rule in this file was written to demonstrate an AI agent in n8n. None of it is Aventura Frontiers' actual pay policy, and none of it comes from a salary survey.

## 1. About this policy

* **Company:** Aventura Frontiers
* **Applies to:** every new hire offer, all locations
* **Currency:** US dollars (USD)
* **Owner:** People team, Aventura Frontiers
* **Approver for offers:** the hiring manager, plus the VP People where section 5 says so
* **Recruiting contact:** people@aventurafrontiers.com

## 2. Grades and base salary ranges

Every role sits in one grade. The base salary in an offer must sit between the minimum and the maximum of that grade.

| Grade | Typical titles | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | Analyst, Coordinator | $60,000 | $70,000 | $80,000 |
| **G2** | Senior Analyst, Specialist | $75,000 | $87,500 | $100,000 |
| **G3** | Manager, Lead | $95,000 | $112,500 | $130,000 |
| **G4** | Senior Manager | $120,000 | $142,500 | $165,000 |
| **G5** | Director | $150,000 | $177,500 | $205,000 |

## 3. One-time and yearly extras

### Joining bonus
A one-time payment in the first paycheck. Optional. It can't go above the cap for the grade.

| Grade | Joining bonus cap |
|---|---|
| G1 | $5,000 |
| G2 | $7,500 |
| G3 | $10,000 |
| G4 | $15,000 |
| G5 | $25,000 |

### Relocation support
A one-time flat amount when the new hire moves for the role.

| Relocation type | Amount |
|---|---|
| **none** | $0 |
| **domestic** (a move within the same country) | $5,000 |
| **international** (a move between countries) | $15,000 |

### Learning and development (L&D) grant
A yearly budget for courses, certifications and conferences. Every offer includes it automatically.

| Grade | Yearly L&D grant |
|---|---|
| G1 | $1,000 |
| G2 | $1,500 |
| G3 | $2,000 |
| G4 | $2,500 |
| G5 | $3,000 |

## 4. Benefits in every offer

* Medical, dental and vision cover from the first day
* 20 days of paid time off a year, plus public holidays
* 401(k) retirement plan with a company match of 4% of base salary
* Hybrid working: three days in the office, two days from home
* A 90-day probation period
* Notice period after probation: 4 weeks from either side

## 5. Approval rules

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Base salary at or below the grade midpoint | The hiring manager approves |
| Base salary above the grade midpoint | The hiring manager **and** the VP People approve |
| Base salary below the minimum or above the maximum | Not allowed. Change the grade or the salary |
| Joining bonus above the grade cap | Not allowed |
| Candidate expects more than the proposed base salary | Flag the gap to the hiring manager. The hiring manager decides |
| Candidate expects more than the grade maximum | Flag that the expectation can't be met in this grade |

## 6. How an offer is made

1. The recruiter gathers the candidate's details, the role, the grade and the proposed package.
2. The package is calculated from this policy. Nobody types a figure in by hand.
3. A draft offer letter is generated from the standard template.
4. The draft goes to the hiring manager for approval. It never goes straight to the candidate.
5. Once approved, the recruiter sends the letter to the candidate.
6. The candidate has 7 days to accept.
